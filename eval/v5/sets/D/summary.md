# What readers told us
The survey had 214 responses.
62% of readers find the weekly digest useful.
71% read the digest on mobile.
Only 28% think the digest is too long.
We interviewed eight readers in October 2026.
One reader said: "I skim the headlines on the train and save the long pieces for Sunday."
Another said: "The charts are the only thing I look at."
Six of the eight readers read the digest on a phone.
Most readers want a daily edition.
A reader told us: "I would pay for a longer version."
Neutral answers ranged from 12% to 30%.
45% of readers disagree that the digest is useful.
